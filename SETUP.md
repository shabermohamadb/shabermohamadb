## ⚡ Design System & Philosophy

Built for maximum creative impact, distinct motion design, and radical minimalism:

- **80% Visual / 20% Text**: Completely purged of wordy paragraphs, generic marketing blurbs, layer badges, and repetitive cards.
- **Massive Typographic Hero (`SHABER.`)**: Oversized brutalist typography, kinetic stroke reveals, rotating mechanical compass, and an infinite horizontal marquee.
- **Animated Technology Stream**: Clean kinetic conveyor ribbon displaying authentic brand logos in their original colors without cards or clutter.
- **Creative Visual Gallery**: 4 strongest projects displayed with bespoke, asymmetric architectural vector artworks.
- **Signature Footer**: Bold typographic statement: `"BUILT WITH CURIOSITY."`.
- **Palette**: Deep Void Black `#060709`, Warm White `#f3f3f6`, Hairline Rules `#181a22`, and a single restrained Electric Blue `#2563eb`.
